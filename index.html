# about

Solutions Architect by trade. Engineer by nature. Generalist by choice.

I'm Craig -- a Senior Solutions Architect at Equinix, where I design and deliver data center and interconnection solutions for enterprises across the Asia-Pacific region. My work sits at the intersection of networking, cloud, and physical infrastructure -- environments where uptime isn't optional and milliseconds matter.

Originally from New Zealand with a degree in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from the University of Canterbury, I moved to Melbourne to chase bigger challenges and better coffee. I bring a first-principles engineering mindset to solving infrastructure problems at scale.

Outside of work, I'm endlessly curious -- I run, cycle, read voraciously, and spend too much time thinking about quantum computing and philosophy. This site is a small corner of the internet where my professional work and personal curiosities coexist.

Melbourne, AU NZ Born BE (Hons) Electrical Dog Dad
